# Client folders

Each client gets a folder in your workspace, optionally cloned from its Azure DevOps repo. **New Client** asks for the repo URL, folder name, org URL, and solutions.

Everything about a client sits under its name in the Lantern view: its solutions; a Plug-ins item with your plug-in projects (matched to what's registered), steps, and traces; PCF controls, tables, system jobs, environment variables, and environment details.
